From pencils to pixels

In Dennis Bransons's article "from Pencils to pixels" one idea that stood out to me was how people often react to ai with fear. Baron explains that technologies that we now consider normal such as computers and typewriters were also once unfamiliar. People worried that these tools would impact our writing and lower our literacy. Over time though these technologies have become so common that we no longer have a fear or distrust with these tools

One example discusses is the typewriter. When typewriters started to become popular people believed it would make writing less personal because it was no longer physically written by hand. Today that concern would seem strange because typing is now a completely normal way to write. Baron's point shows that our reactions to technology change often once we become more comfortable with it. I think we have the same reaction today with AI. Programs that can generate or revise writing have caused concerns about whether or not people are doing their own works in school and jobs. In school specifically people worry that children have become to dependant on AI instead of developing their own critical thinking schools

I do agree that we should not be completely dependant on AI to do all of our work however i do believe that it can be helpful if you use it just as a tool. Generative AI definetly can and has been misused especially when someone claims AI generated work to be their own. Barons article suggests that instead of immediately rejecting new tools, we should learn to use them responsibly. Just as computers became writing tools, AI may become another tool that changes how we write rather than just replacing it

My favorite Hobby

There is something about baking that I have always found so comforitng. No matter what im making, I like how baking gives me something to focus on while also being able to express my creativity. Although sometimes it can be a little stressful when things dont turn out perfect or exactly that way I imagine it.

One of my favorite things about baking is how everything starts out as completely seperate things and being able to create something entirely new. I also love being able to give my friends and family whatever I make.

It has also taught me that making mistakes isnt always a bad thing. I've definetly made things that gone look great, but most of the time they still taste good. Everytime I bake something, I learn something new and I can learn what different techniques work and what things I need to change next time. I think that why it is so enjoyable for me. You dont have to be perfect at everything for it to still be fun.
